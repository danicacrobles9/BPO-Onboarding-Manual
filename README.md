# 🏢 Corporate Onboarding & Training Guide (Mock Manual)
**Document Version:** 1.0 (2026)  
**Prepared By:** HR Department / Training & Development Team

Welcome to the Team! This document serves as the official handbook to guide new remote and on-site customer service professionals through their first week of operational training.

---

## 📅 Week 1: Training Agenda

### Day 1: New Hire Orientation
* **09:00 AM - 11:00 AM:** Virtual Welcome, Company Culture & Mission Overview.
* **11:00 AM - 12:30 PM:** Pre-Employment Requirements Verification & System IT setups.
* **01:30 PM - 05:00 PM:** Introduction to Communication Standards and KPI Metrics.

### Day 2 to Day 5: Process & Back-Office Training
* **Customer Ticket Management:** Handling account refunds, processing standard cancellations, and billing corrections.
* **Communication Pipelines:** Professional email drafting standards and internal corporate communication protocols.

---

## 📜 Employee Compliance Checklist

Every new hire must upload digital copies of the following documents to the HR Portal within their first week:
[ ] Signed Employment Contract
[ ] Valid Government ID (e.g., SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG)
[ ] Barangay Clearance / NBI Clearance
[ ] Solo Parent Identification Card *(if applicable for flexible shift options)*

---

## ⚖️ Core Workplace Policies

### 🏠 Remote Work & Back-Office Integrity
Employees handling back-office tasks (e.g., sensitive customer data, processing financial refunds) must maintain a secure, private workstation environment. Sharing of system access or account credentials strictly violates client security agreements.

### 📈 Performance Tracking (KPIs)
Performance will be assessed monthly based on regular metrics:
1. **Average Handle Time (AHT):** Resolving customer concerns cleanly and efficiently.
2. **Quality Assurance (QA):** Maintaining polite, precise communication standards in emails.
